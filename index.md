# The Definitive Guide and Wiki to Titanfall 2 

## Welcome to Frontier Biz. Whether you are a fresh Pilot just starting out a veteran pilot looking to break through a skill plateau, you’ve found the right terminal.

The guide and wiki is designed with a focus on the most optimal and high-level play. While we cover universal mechanics applicable to all levels, please assume all strategies are optimized for the master and insane difficulties on campaign and frontier defense unless otherwise specified.

{: .note } 
**Image Quality:** To ensure fast loading, some thumbnails may appear soft. Click or tap any image to view it in full resolution.

### Collaborative Intelligence
The Frontier is always changing. If you have factual data to contribute, we invite you to join our community.

Frontier biz has a Discord for those wishing to join, You can apply to join via our Discord link. Please note that there is an interview process to gain access. This is to maintain the quality of the discord.

If you already have access, head to the #guide-discussions channel to submit edits or new findings. Be prepared to defend your data with evidence as we value rigorous debate to ensure our guides remain the best in the community.

Perspective is everything. Two Pilots can look at the same map and see two different paths to victory. Be respectful, stay sharp, and have fun.
